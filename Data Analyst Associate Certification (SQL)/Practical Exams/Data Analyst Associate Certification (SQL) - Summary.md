# Data Analyst Associate Certification (SQL) - Practical Exam

This certification exam (_DataCamp_) performed various tasks within SQL including exploratory analysis, identifying & fixing inconsistencies, & data aggregation. The dataset, contained in a single table, revolved around the sales of a fictional grocery store chain.  
This exam was taken & completed: March, 2025.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Background:** FoodYum is a grocery store chain that is based in the United States. FoodYum sells items such as produce, meat, dairy, baked goods, snacks, and other household food staples. As food costs rise, FoodYum wants to make sure it keeps stocking products in all categories that cover a range of prices to ensure they have stock for a broad range of customers.


### Brief Summary
With a diverse collection of customers, FoodYum has a strong motivation to understand their purchase patterns so that they can effectively maintain & restock the items that are consumed. As a grocery store chain with establishments across the US, FoodYum will have to balance different customer needs & desires with different resources in terms of the availability of different products. For example, one store in the northeast US may value fish, meat, & dairy more highly than a store in the south depending on consumption levels & purchase patterns of their respective customers. Additionally, it may be more challenging for certain locations to obtain certain products like certain fish or meats.  
Moreover, FoodYum prioritizes having diversity amongst their products so that customers, many of which may possess different desires or restrictions when it comes to groceries & consumables, can still have a wide range of access when shopping. In other words, FoodYum wants to ensure that people with dietary (or other) restrictions still have access to foods they want & need (e.g. gluten free). Furthermore, FoodYum wants to enable people & their access to such items & brands by setting ranges of prices across item categories. Therefore, people with different budgets will still have options to choose from.

By maintaining a robust stock of items in terms of nutrition, brands, & pricing, FoodYum can optimize their ability to meet & respond to the demands of their diverse customer base across the United States.

The dataset utilized covered products in stock & associated details. Regarding the analyses, there were four main tasks involved.
- **Task 1** addressed an inconsistency having to do with the year in which products were first introduced to FoodYum's stock. Fortunately, this issue, which related to ten percent of the dataset, was contained within a specific year (2022) & so an easy fix could be applied.
- The second task fixed the inconsistency from **Task 1** & all others contained in the dataset including other missing values, inconsistencies, & incorrect data types. This helped ensure effective, credible analyses in following sections.
- The third task evaluated the price ranges of each product type to assess the diversity of costs that customers face. This process can help quantify & compare the overall affordability of the product types, how it might influence how customers approach their shopping, & how many products might be sold.
- The fourth & final task looked at some of the most popular meat & dairy items that FoodYum has recently sold. Of the 1,700 unique products in stock, 698 of them are dairy or meat items that have sold more than ten units per month on average. Across the five product types that FoodYum offers, these particular dairy & meat items account for more than 40 percent of their products, indicating that customers seem particularly interested in them. As such, restocking these specific items is especially important for FoodYum going forward.
	- Further analyses could evaluate the sales of these specific items across the four different warehouse locations from which FoodYum's stock originates.
